# Project Methodology

## Agile/Scrum

Our team follows an Agile methodology, organizing our work into standard sprint cycles to iteratively deliver features and improvements.

## Work Tracking

We use a Gitea Kanban Project Board to organize tasks and track team velocity. Our board is structured with the following columns:

- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- In Progress
- Review & Testing
- Done

## Stakeholder Interaction

We hold weekly review meetings with our assigned tutor (who serves as our "client"). During these meetings, we discuss project requirements, evaluate the current MVP progress, and integrate client feedback directly into our upcoming sprints.
