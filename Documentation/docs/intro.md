# Olympiad Portal Documentation

The **Olympiad Portal** is a web platform for running academic olympiads end to end: organisers create olympiads and schedule rounds, schools and their educators register entrants, students sit online or paper-based assessments, and marking, results, certificates and appeals are handled inside the platform.

This site documents the project's full lifecycle, from initial planning through to testing and delivery.
