# Sprint 2 Stakeholder & User Feedback

**Date**: September 2026
**Participants**: Development Team, University Stakeholders (Product Owner), and Early Test Users.
**Focus**: Review of Sprint 1 MVP and planning for Sprint 2 UI/UX direction.

## Feedback Collection and Evaluation

During the formal Sprint 1 review and subsequent user testing sessions, we actively solicited feedback on the initial design and usability of the platform. The feedback was formally collected and evaluated by the team to ensure our methodology directly served stakeholder needs.

### The Core Feedback: "Generic and Clunky"

The most significant and consistent piece of feedback received was regarding the visual identity and layout of the portal.

During the Sprint 1 review, stakeholders and early test users noted that the UI felt too much like a generic SaaS template, specifically citing the heavy use of drop-shadow cards and padded containers as clunky. The university stakeholders emphasized that the platform needs to feel authoritative and academic, rather than like a standard startup application.

### Evaluation and Integration (The UI Pivot)

We evaluated this feedback as high priority, as it directly impacted the perceived professionalism of the platform.

To integrate this feedback, we executed a major UI pivot in Sprint 2: we stripped out all card wrappers and standard borders, completely redesigning the portal into a bespoke, flat, edge-to-edge "Syllabus" layout with alternating color washes. This aligns the platform with the authoritative, academic brand identity required by the university.

This pivot serves as primary evidence of our extensive stakeholder interaction and our commitment to actively integrating user feedback into the development cycle.

## Secondary Feedback

- **Dynamic School Selection**: Users requested a faster way to invite schools to rounds. This was integrated into Sprint 2 via a dynamic search and invitation feature.
- **Image Support in Questions**: Educators noted that many mathematics and science questions require diagrams. We prioritized building an advanced question builder with image upload support for Sprint 2.
